# C7 Overwatch Design System

Derived from the Cyber 7 Group brand identity — a cybersecurity professional services firm.

## Tokens

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| navy-950 | `#0a1628` | Page background |
| navy-900 | `#142454` | Card backgrounds |
| surface | `#0f1a2e` | Panel background |
| surface-light | `#162238` | Hover states |
| surface-lighter | `#1e2d48` | Selected states |
| surface-border | `#2a3a5c` | Borders, dividers |
| cyber-600 | `#00c0f4` | Primary accent, buttons |
| cyber-400 | `#26cbf6` | Text accents, links |
| alert-red | `#ef4444` | Destructive, overdue |
| alert-amber | `#f59e0b` | Warnings, waiting |
| alert-green | `#22c55e` | Success, completed |

### Typography

- **Headings**: Inter, 600–700 weight, tracking tight
- **Body**: Inter, 400–500 weight, 1.6 line-height
- **Mono**: JetBrains Mono for code, invoice numbers, IDs
- Scale: 12 14 16 18 20 24 32px

### Spacing

- Grid base: 4px
- Scale: 4 8 12 16 20 24 32 40 48

### Radius

- Cards: 12px (rounded-xl)
- Inputs: 10px (rounded-lg)
- Buttons: 10px
- Badges: full (rounded-full)

### Motion

- Duration: 150–250ms
- Easing: ease-out (standard), no bounce/elastic
- prefers-reduced-motion: disables all animations
